## Terms of Use & Non-Commercial Research License

The dataset contains `<user, artist, song, timestamp>` scrobble tuples collected via the Last.fm API (`user.getRecentTracks`) representing the complete listening habits (up to May 5th, 2009) for 992 users.

The official terms distributed with `README.txt` in the archive state:
